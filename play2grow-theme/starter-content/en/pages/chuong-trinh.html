Choosing the right class for your child

  • Start from your child’s interests. Do they love drawing, music or moving around? Early enthusiasm makes children want to come back every week.
  • Check the age group and schedule. Pick a time that fits your family’s routine so your child can attend regularly.
  • There’s no need to sign up for too many classes at once. One or two classes your child truly enjoys are usually easier to keep up than a packed timetable.
  • Talk to the centre. Ask what a typical lesson looks like, how big the class is and how parents are kept informed about their child’s progress.
