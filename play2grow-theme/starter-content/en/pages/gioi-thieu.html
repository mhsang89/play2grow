Who we are

Play2Grow is a centre for children offering STEAM English alongside art, music and dance classes. It is built on a simple belief: English feels closest to children when it is connected to what they are doing, seeing and curious about. Instead of keeping the language inside textbooks, Play2Grow brings English into games, into hands-on discovery in the classroom and into the everyday rhythm of family life. Our art, music and dance classes give children more chances to try new things, find what they love and express themselves with confidence.

The name says it all: play to grow. In every game, every “what’s this?” and every time children make something with their own hands, they learn a little more – about the world around them and about how to put their thoughts into words.

Our mission

To help children approach English as a tool for exploring the world – through joyful, meaningful learning experiences connected to everyday life – and to help parents feel confident supporting their children along the way.

Our vision

To be a trusted companion for families in nurturing children’s love of language and their eagerness to learn, where every day can become a chance to learn something new.

Why learn English through experience?

Many children can recite a list of words but hesitate when they need those same words to ask a simple question. The gap between “knowing” and “using” often comes from having too few chances to use the language in situations that matter to them.

When a new word comes with an experience – holding an orange, smelling it, counting the segments and saying “orange” – it is no longer just a symbol on paper. It is linked to images, feelings and the fun of that moment. Play2Grow starts from experience because we believe it makes English feel familiar, natural and worth using.

This does not mean structured learning is less important. Vocabulary, pronunciation, grammar, reading and writing all need to be built step by step. Learning through experience is the essential complement: the place where what children have learnt gets put to use.

Our educational philosophy

Start with curiosity

A child’s question is the best starting point for a lesson. When a child wants to know “what is this called in English?”, learning happens because the child needs it, not because they were told to.

Context first, memorising later

Words and phrases are introduced in a real situation – a meal, a walk, a game – so children understand when and why to use them before they are asked to remember them.

Learning with the whole body and all the senses

Young children learn by looking, listening, touching, moving and doing. The more an activity involves the senses, the more “anchors” the language has for children to recall it.

It’s fine to make mistakes and try again

Making mistakes is a normal part of learning a new language. A safe environment without pressure to score helps children speak up – which matters far more than speaking perfectly from day one.

Little and often beats long and intense

Ten happy minutes a day is usually worth more than one long, tiring session a week. Language progress takes time, and every child has their own pace.

Responsible use of technology

Play2Grow puts real classroom experiences first. Whenever we use technology – in class or in future learning tools, including artificial intelligence (AI) – we follow these principles:

  • Technology serves real learning; it does not replace children’s time for play, movement and face-to-face conversation.
  • We respect age-appropriate screen-time guidance and encourage off-screen activities.
  • We collect as little data as possible, especially children’s data, and explain clearly what it is used for.
  • We are transparent about which tools are actually in use and which are only planned.
  • We never present AI as “magic”. Any AI feature, if offered, must be described accurately, including its limits.
