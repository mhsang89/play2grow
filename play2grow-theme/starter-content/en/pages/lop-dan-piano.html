The piano class introduces children to music through a familiar instrument: listening, feeling the rhythm, reading notes and coordinating both hands. Starting with simple tunes, children gradually learn to play a whole piece and share that joy with their family.

Learning an instrument takes perseverance. Regular practice at home – even a few minutes a day – often matters as much as time spent in class.
