Cutting, gluing, folding, modelling, building – the arts and crafts class gives children the chance to turn simple materials into something of their own. Making things by hand teaches children to come up with an idea, follow steps and patiently finish a piece of work.

Craft activities often help children develop fine motor skills, the ability to follow instructions and the confidence that comes from finishing something themselves.
