This policy explains what information Play2Grow collects when you use this website, why we collect it, how we store and protect it, and your rights over your personal data. If there is any difference between language versions, the Vietnamese version prevails.

1. Scope

This policy applies to this website and the forms on it. Other Play2Grow services may have their own policies, which will be provided when you use them.

2. Who is responsible for your data

3. Information we collect

Information you give us through the contact form

  • Name of the parent or contact person.
  • Phone number and/or email address (only one of them is required).
  • The topic you are interested in (chosen from a list, optional).
  • Your child’s age group (optional, age range only).
  • Your message (optional).
  • The time you ticked the consent box and the page you sent the form from.

The form does not ask for a child’s name, photo, date of birth or any other identifying information about the child. The website theme does not store your IP address or browser information with your enquiry. To prevent automated spam, the system briefly records the number of submissions from a connection in a one-way encrypted form for about 10 minutes, after which it is deleted automatically.

Comments (if comments are enabled)

When you leave a comment, WordPress records your name, email, comment text, IP address and browser information to help detect spam. Your email address is not published.

Technical information

The hosting provider’s servers may keep access logs (for example IP address, time of visit, pages viewed) according to their configuration, for operation and security purposes.

4. Information about children

This website is intended for parents and carers. The contact form is for parents, guardians or people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect personal data about children through this website. If you find that a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

5. How we use information

  • To reply to your questions or requests.
  • To send information related to the topics you chose, such as class start dates, programs and activities at Play2Grow.
  • To protect the website against spam and abuse.

We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for purposes other than those above without your consent.

6. Legal basis: your consent

Information is only sent through the form when you tick the consent box. You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us; this does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal.

7. Storage and retention

Information sent through the form is stored in the website’s database, can only be viewed by authorised administrator accounts, and may be forwarded to Play2Grow’s internal mailbox for handling. Enquiries are deleted automatically after [p2g_retention] from the date of submission, unless the law requires them to be kept longer.

8. Sharing information

We only share information:

  • With technical service providers needed to run the website (hosting, email delivery), to the extent necessary to provide the service.
  • When required by a competent authority in accordance with the law.

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Be informed about the processing of your personal data.
  • Access, view and request correction of the information you provided.
  • Request deletion of your information or restriction of processing.
  • Withdraw your consent.
  • Make a complaint in accordance with the law.

To exercise these rights, please send a request via the Contact page, stating the email address or phone number you used. We may need to verify your identity before acting on the request, to protect your own data.

10. Cookies

The website theme does not set tracking, advertising or analytics cookies. WordPress uses a few essential technical cookies:

  • Login cookies for administrators and registered users.
  • If you comment and choose to save your details, a cookie that remembers your name and email for next time.

If the website adds analytics, advertising or third-party embedded content in the future, this policy will be updated and you will be asked for consent where the law requires it.

11. Security

We apply appropriate technical measures such as HTTPS encryption, limiting access to enquiry data to administrators, and anti-spam protection for forms. However, no method of transmission or storage on the Internet is completely secure.

12. Legal references

This policy was prepared with reference to personal data protection regulations in Vietnam, including Decree 13/2023/ND-CP and the Law on Personal Data Protection.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website changes the way it handles data. The new version will be published on this page with the date of the update.
