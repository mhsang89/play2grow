"Play2Grow enquiries"
"The contact / enquiry form of the Play2Grow Core plugin collects information from parents or contact persons: full name, phone number and/or email, topic of interest, the child’s age group (optional, age range only) and the message. The form does not ask for a child’s name, photo or identifying information. Data is stored in the website’s database, can only be viewed by administrators, and is sent to the notification email set by the administrator. The theme does not store IP addresses or browser information with enquiries. Data is deleted automatically after the retention period set in Settings → Play2Grow."
