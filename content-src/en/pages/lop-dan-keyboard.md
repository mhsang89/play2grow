<!--meta Keyboard class (EN) -->
An electronic keyboard has many built-in sounds and accompaniment rhythms, so children can play familiar melodies and enjoy music from the very first lessons. In class, children get to know rhythm, where the keys are, how to read notes and how to coordinate their hands.

As with any instrument, regular practice helps children progress and feel more confident when they perform.

:::note
Mô tả chung – bổ sung độ tuổi, thời lượng, giáo viên, khác biệt giữa lớp Piano và Keyboard (xem bản tiếng Việt).
:::
