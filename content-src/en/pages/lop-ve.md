<!--meta Drawing class (EN) -->
Drawing is a natural way for children to tell us about their world, even before they can write. In the drawing class at Play2Grow, children observe, try different materials and create pictures from their own ideas.

Drawing often helps children develop fine motor skills, observation and concentration, and gives them a way to express feelings and ideas. Every child moves at their own pace; what matters is having the chance to try, create and be encouraged.

:::note
Mô tả chung – bổ sung độ tuổi, chất liệu, chủ đề, học liệu, giáo viên (xem bản tiếng Việt).
:::

## Who is this class for?

Age group, schedule and fees are shown under **Quick facts**. The class suits children who love colours and enjoy making things of their own.

## Tips for parents

:::checklist
- Ask your child about the story in the picture instead of only saying “how beautiful”.
- Set aside a small corner at home to display your child’s artwork.
- Encourage experimenting – it doesn’t have to look “real”.
:::
