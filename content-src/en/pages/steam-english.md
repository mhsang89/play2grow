**STEAM** stands for Science, Technology, Engineering, Art and Mathematics. The STEAM English class at Play2Grow combines English with hands-on activities across these areas: English is not just a subject but the language children use to observe, ask questions, experiment, build something and explain what they did.

This is also how Play2Grow understands [learning English through experience]({{page:phuong-phap-hoc}}): words and phrases are tied to something concrete the child is doing, so there is a real reason to listen, understand and speak.

:::note
Mô tả chung dựa trên tên chương trình (bản tiếng Anh). Bổ sung mục tiêu, giáo trình, giáo viên, ví dụ dự án – xem ghi chú bản tiếng Việt.
:::

## What can children experience in STEAM English?

STEAM-style activities typically include:

:::checklist
- Observing and doing simple experiments, then describing the results in English.
- Building models in groups and talking it through with short phrases.
- Counting, measuring and comparing – meeting English through maths ideas.
- Presenting their work to the class at the end of the lesson.
:::

:::note
Các dạng hoạt động STEAM phổ biến – sửa cho đúng hoạt động thực tế trước khi xuất bản.
:::
